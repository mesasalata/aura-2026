<Section id="overview" title="Integrated HP" kicker="01">

Integrated Human Practices means feedback did not sit in a report - it changed the siphon, the flag, the claims, and safety commitments. This page traces specific stakeholder input to specific design moves.

</Section>

<Section id="farmer" title="Farmers → inline siphon" kicker="02">

**Heard.** A test must sit in the milking line, cost less than the loss it prevents, and return a readout before the tank is mixed.

**Integrated change.** We put AURA in the milking machine as an inline siphon - not another lab errand - and made the output a quiet flag, not a diagnosis.

</Section>

<Section id="vet" title="Veterinarians → diagnostic-support framing" kicker="03">

**Heard.** Early signal is good; mistaken diagnosis is dangerous. Treatments should be targeted to the root problem, not blanket antibiotics.

**Integrated change.** All wiki copy positions AURA as decision-support pointing to confirmatory testing. No cure or diagnosis claims. miR-223 was chosen in part because it can differentiate bacterial from other causes.

</Section>

<Section id="processor" title="Processors → tank-level thinking" kicker="04">

**Heard.** Milk quality and SCC already drive pricing.

**Integrated change.** We added cow-level and tank-level decision framing: flag this cow before the tank is mixed.

</Section>

<Section id="regulator" title="Regulators → containment by design" kicker="05">

**Heard.** Engineered systems must stay contained.

**Integrated change.** DNA catapult on a FET surface, documented waste handling, no field release this season. See Safety page.

</Section>

<Section id="consumer" title="Consumers → transparency" kicker="06">

**Heard.** People want safe milk and well-treated animals; GMO language is often misunderstood.

**Integrated change.** Education materials use analogies first; welfare and milk safety are explicit. The device is an inline transistor, not a living circuit in the cow.

</Section>

<Section id="loop" title="The feedback loop" kicker="07">

<Callout variant="note" title="Ongoing">
  HP is not finished when interviews end. Each engineering cycle includes a check: does this still match what stakeholders said?
</Callout>

</Section>
