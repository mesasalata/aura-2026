<Section id="overview" title="Human practices overview" kicker="01">

AURA only matters if the people who would use it would trust it. Human Practices is how we listened - to farmers, vets, processors, regulators, and consumers - and how that listening changed the design.

</Section>

<Section id="approach" title="Our approach" kicker="02">

We ran semi-structured interviews and follow-ups across the dairy value chain. Themes were coded anonymously and mapped to engineering decisions. We did not treat HP as a separate essay - it is part of how the biosensor was scoped.

</Section>

<Section id="themes" title="Cross-cutting themes" kicker="04">

- **Trust through routine** - fits milking, not the lab
- **Honest scope** - screen, not diagnosis; antibiotics not automatic
- **Containment** - no environmental release of engineered material
- **Plain language** - explain synbio without jargon or fear

</Section>

<Section id="outreach" title="Outreach" kicker="05">
