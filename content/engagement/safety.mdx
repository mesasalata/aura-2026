<Section id="organisms" title="Organisms & parts" kicker="03">

Work uses standard chassis strains at approved biosafety levels. No pathogenic mastitis organisms are cultured for device testing; spiked panels use safe surrogates or purified markers.

<Callout variant="safety" title="No release">
  AURA is not deployed in animals or the environment this season. Engineered material is contained, inactivated for disposal, and never released deliberately.
