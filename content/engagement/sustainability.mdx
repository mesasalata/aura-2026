<Section id="overview" title="Sustainability" kicker="01">

AURA connects animal welfare, economic resilience for farmers, and responsible use of antibiotics. Sustainability here means the long-term health of animals, farms, and the environment - not greenwashing.

</Section>

<Section id="welfare" title="Animal welfare" kicker="02">

Earlier detection of subclinical mastitis can reduce progression to painful clinical disease and support targeted care. Welfare framing is central, not a side note - sick cows are both an ethical and economic problem.

</Section>

<Section id="antibiotics" title="Antimicrobial stewardship" kicker="03">

Vets stressed that screens must not drive automatic antibiotic use. AURA is designed to flag risk for follow-up - look at this cow, consider confirmatory testing, skip blanket treatment of the herd.

</Section>

<Section id="environment" title="Environmental impact" kicker="04">

- **Inline FET** - DNA on a transistor surface, no deliberate release of engineered organisms
- **Reduced discard milk** - earlier action could lower waste at herd level if validated
- **Consumables** - siphon chamber and electrodes designed for the life of the milking machine, with minimal upkeep

</Section>
