<Section id="overview" title="Sustainable Development Goals" kicker="01">

AURA connects synthetic biology with sustainable agriculture by aiming to detect bovine mastitis earlier during routine milking. Our project relates most strongly to SDGs 2, 3, 9, and 12 through its potential to support animal health, protect milk production, integrate biosensing into agricultural infrastructure, and reduce avoidable resource losses.

Bovine mastitis affects animal health, milk production, and farm profitability. Global economic losses associated with mastitis have been estimated at up to US$32 billion annually.<sup>[1,2]</sup> AURA aims to contribute to this challenge through a reusable, cell-free biosensor targeting miR-223, with the long-term goal of integrating earlier mastitis screening into routine milking.

</Section>

<Section id="sdg2" title="SDG 2: Zero Hunger" kicker="02">

### Supporting resilient dairy production

SDG 2 promotes food security and sustainable agriculture, including resilient production systems.<sup>[3]</sup>

Mastitis can reduce milk yield and contribute to discarded milk, making disease management important for efficient dairy production.<sup>[2]</sup> Earlier screening during routine milking could help farmers identify cows requiring attention before disease progresses further.

This may be particularly useful in larger herds, where continuous observation of every individual animal is difficult. By supporting earlier identification of potential cases, AURA could contribute to more resilient dairy production and reduced production losses.

</Section>

<Section id="sdg3" title="SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being" kicker="03">

### Supporting responsible health management

Mastitis is an inflammatory disease that can negatively affect dairy-cow health and welfare. Subclinical cases may develop without obvious visible symptoms, making early identification difficult.

AURA is intended as a screening and decision-support tool. An earlier warning could prompt further investigation and support more timely health-management decisions.

Its connection to SDG 3 is therefore through supporting responsible animal-health monitoring and, indirectly, more informed use of veterinary treatment.<sup>[4]</sup>

</Section>

<Section id="sdg9" title="SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure" kicker="04">

### Integrating biosensing into dairy infrastructure

SDG 9 promotes innovation and technological development.<sup>[5]</sup>

AURA’s long-term design combines molecular biosensing, electronic detection, and software within the normal milking workflow. Our aim is to integrate biological monitoring into existing agricultural infrastructure without adding unnecessary complexity to the milking workflow.

This represents a broader shift from laboratory-only molecular diagnostics toward integrated agricultural biosensing.

</Section>

<Section id="sdg12" title="SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production" kicker="05">

### Reducing avoidable losses

SDG 12 promotes efficient use of resources and more sustainable production.<sup>[6]</sup>

Mastitis can lead to reduced milk yield and wasted production inputs such as feed, water, energy, land, and labour. Earlier detection could help reduce some of these avoidable losses by allowing animals to receive attention sooner.

AURA is also being designed with sensor reuse in mind, which could reduce material consumption compared with single-use testing approaches.

</Section>

<Section id="bigger-picture" title="The bigger picture" kicker="06">

AURA connects these four SDGs through one central goal: making mastitis monitoring earlier, more integrated, and more efficient.

- **SDG 2:** protecting dairy productivity
- **SDG 3:** supporting animal-health monitoring
- **SDG 9:** integrating biosensing into farm infrastructure
- **SDG 12:** reducing production and material losses

Our current work represents an early step toward this vision. The complete inline system will require further validation in raw milk and integration with the final electronic sensing platform.

</Section>
