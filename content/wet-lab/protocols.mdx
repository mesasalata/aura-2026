<Section id="preparation" title="Workspace and oligo preparation" kicker="02">

### RNase-free workspace

1. Wipe benchtop, pipettes, and tube racks with RNase AWAY, then rinse with nuclease-free water. Let air dry.
2. Use filter tips throughout. Wear gloves; change after touching shared surfaces.
3. Label all tubes before filling. Wrap every FAM-labelled tube in foil immediately.
4. Keep a dedicated DNA-only zone, with no shared pipettes between beacon and target tubes.

### Resuspend oligonucleotides

Resuspension volume (µL) = nmol delivered × 10, giving a 100 µM stock.

1. Centrifuge the sealed shipping tube at 500×g for 30 s before opening.
2. Add the calculated buffer volume slowly against the tube wall.
3. Vortex 10 s, incubate at 37 °C for 5 min, then vortex 10 s again.
4. Aliquot 10 µL single-use portions into amber tubes. Store at −20 °C.
5. Make 10 µM working stocks: 10 µL of 100 µM stock + 90 µL matching buffer.

</Section>

<Section id="refold" title="Hairpin refolding and beacon QC" kicker="03">

### Refold the hairpin beacon

1. Add 960 µL of 1× binding buffer to a low-binding 1.5 mL amber tube.
2. Add 240 µL of 10 µM beacon working stock (giving 1,200 µL at 1 µM).
3. Mix by gentle inversion 10×.
4. Thermocycler: 95 °C × 5 min, ramp to 25 °C at 0.1 °C/s (~11.7 min), hold 25 °C × 5 min, then hold at 4 °C. As a fallback, heat to 95 °C × 5 min on a heat block, turn the block off, and cool passively on the bench.
5. Wrap in foil. Use within 24 h if stored at 4 °C, or aliquot and freeze at −20 °C.
6. On the assay day, dilute the 1 µM refolded stock 1:5 in 1× binding buffer to make 200 nM.

<Section id="assay" title="Fluorescence binding assay" kicker="04">

1. Prepare probe and target/control solutions in 1× PBS with 5 mM MgCl₂, at 200 nM each (2× working concentration).
2. Dispense 20 µL probe mix and 20 µL target/control mix into each well, for a final volume of **40 µL** with approximately **100 nM probe and 100 nM target** where applicable.
3. Prepare at least three technical replicates per condition.
4. Include a no-target control, a complementary positive target and a background (water) blank.
5. Measure FAM fluorescence using consistent excitation, emission and gain settings, at a constant temperature, at fixed time intervals.
6. Keep individual replicate values before calculating the mean.
7. Calculate mean RFU, SD, SNR and fluorescence change as required, and compare target-containing samples with matched controls rather than interpreting RFU alone.
8. Report the instrument settings and whether any blank subtraction or calibration was performed.

<Section id="temperature" title="Temperature-performance assay at 39 °C" kicker="05">

1. Prepare the sensor-side and target/control-side working mixes at 200 nM in 1× PBS/5 mM MgCl₂; this gives 100 nM of each intended strand after mixing equal 20 µL volumes.
2. Dispense 20 µL sensor mix and 20 µL target/control mix into each assigned well. Use three technical replicates per condition.
3. Include: v2 sensor + water, v2 + miR223_DNA, v2 + miR21_DNA, 3′-end-shortened sensor + water, 3′-end-shortened sensor + miR223_DNA, and a water blank.
4. Begin FAM reads immediately after mixing. Acquire a time-zero read and repeated measurements for a recorded 30 min hold at 39 °C.
5. Subtract the water blank. Retain each technical replicate, calculate ΔF from time 0, and calculate fold-change relative to the matched sensor-only control.

</Section>

<Section id="rnaseh" title="RNase H reset" kicker="06">

1. Prepare the miRNA-223 probe reactions using **three technical replicates per condition**, with a final volume of **40 µL per well** and probe and target at **100 nM** where applicable.
2. Prepare RNA-containing samples with **synthetic miRNA-223**, since RNase H acts on RNA–DNA hybrids. Include **ssDNA miR-223 and no-target samples** as reference conditions.
3. For the RNA conditions assigned to RNase H treatment, add **0.4 µL RNase H per reaction** (Thermo Scientific EN0201, 5 U/µL; approximately 2 U per well) in 1× buffer with 5 mM MgCl₂. Add it near the top of the well and centrifuge the plate to mix.
4. Start measuring immediately (we had a delay of about 1 minute). Measure fluorescence at approximately **39 °C** over **30 minutes**, at **0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 minutes**.
5. Compare the change in fluorescence of the **miRNA-223 + RNase H** samples with the ssDNA reference and no-target traces.
6. To test whether the probe can respond again, add fresh **ssDNA miR-223** to the treated samples and measure for a further 30 minutes.

<Callout title="Control to add">

Our ssDNA reference was not treated with RNase H. Including an RNase H-treated DNA reference would show how much of the signal decrease comes specifically from enzymatic RNA degradation.

</Callout>

</Section>

<Section id="strand-displacement" title="Toehold-mediated strand displacement" kicker="07">

1. Prepare the selected miR-223 probe with either **ssDNA miR-223 or miRNA-223** and the appropriate **9 bp or 13 bp biotinylated replacer strand**, using three technical replicates per condition.
2. Measure fluorescence **before bead treatment** to obtain a pre-bead reference measurement.
3. Add **magnetic streptavidin beads** to the samples designated for bead treatment (approximately **0.8 µL of beads per well**).
4. Wash the bead-containing samples **three times**, using **40 µL of wash buffer** (1× PBS, 5 mM MgCl₂) per wash.
5. Resuspend the beads after washing and add **sensorResetter** to the appropriate conditions, to a final concentration of approximately **110 nM**.
6. For the re-detection conditions, add fresh **ssDNA miR-223 or miRNA-223**. Control conditions receive water instead.
7. Measure fluorescence and compare it with the corresponding pre-bead and no-bead reference conditions.

<Callout title="What we would change">

Many beads were likely lost during our washes, which made the result inconclusive. Increasing or optimizing bead quantity, improving bead retention during washing, and adding controls that distinguish molecular resetting from physical sensor loss should come first.

</Callout>
