<Section id="overview" title="Parts overview" kicker="01">

AURA parts are DNA sequences for the catapult on the FET gate - invasion regions complementary to bta-miR-223, plus off-target controls. Registry IDs will be added on submission; sequences and intended use are documented here first.

<Callout variant="note" title="Characterisation">
  Parts without measurement data are labelled <em>uncharacterised</em>. Do not assume performance from sequence alone.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section id="basic" title="Basic parts" kicker="02">

| Part | Type | Status | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AuraCat-stem | DNA stem | Uncharacterised | Surface-tethered stem that stays closed until invasion |
| AuraCat-inv | Invasion region | Testing | Complementary to bta-miR-223 |
| AuraCat-off | Off-target control | Designed | Invasion region for an irrelevant RNA |
| AuraRef-probe | Electrode probe | Testing | Ionic / electrochemical baseline upstream of the chamber |

<Section id="composite" title="Composite parts" kicker="03">

Composite sensing units combine the stem, invasion region, and FET-surface chemistry. Assembly is oligo synthesis and surface attachment, not a living circuit.

| Composite | Components | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AuraCatapult-v1 | stem + miR-223 invasion region | In design |
| AuraCatapult-v2 | tuned stem length | In design |
| AuraGate-FET | catapult + gate chemistry | Pending |

</Section>
