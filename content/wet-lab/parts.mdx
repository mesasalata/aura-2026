<Section id="overview" title="Overview" kicker="01">

AURA’s parts are short synthetic DNA oligonucleotides rather than genetic constructs. Four are miR-223-responsive catapult probes, labelled with a 5′ BHQ-1 quencher and a 3′ 6-FAM fluorophore so that opening of the hairpin separates the quencher and fluorophore and produces an increase in fluorescence. The other three are strands for the strand-displacement reset strategy.

</Section>

In our fluorescence assay against a DNA analogue of miR-223, the signal-to-noise ratios were 6.73 (BBa_26PD7O10), 15.59 (BBa_26IRC2X4), 11.81 (BBa_268GQMMU) and 17.96 (BBa_26U24IU9). BBa_26U24IU9 was selected for later experiments, where it responded to synthetic miR-223 RNA at 39 °C. The characterization is described on our Experiments and Measurement pages.

</Section>
