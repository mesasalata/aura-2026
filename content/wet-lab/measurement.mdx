<Section id="overview" title="Measurement overview" kicker="01">

Characterisation turns the inline FET into comparable data: how far the current ticks, how noisy the milk is, and when a residual becomes a flag. This page collects calibration methods and results as they land.

</Section>

<Section id="fet" title="FET current" kicker="02">

**Setup.** Source–drain current recorded while milk (or buffer) washes the gated catapult surface; blank with unspiked matrix.

**Metrics.**
- Baseline current with the catapult closed
- ΔI when miR-223 opens the invasion-region stem
- Residual after subtracting that sample's reference-electrode baseline

<Section id="electrodes" title="Reference electrodes" kicker="03">

Ionic concentration and electrochemical activity are read upstream of the chamber, so salty milk is less likely to raise a false flag. Calibration uses known-salt standards in milk matrix.

<Section id="calibration" title="Calibration standards" kicker="04">

Spiked miR-223 stocks at known concentration anchor the load score. Standards are prepared fresh or thawed from documented aliquots with chain-of-custody in the notebook.

</Section>

<Section id="units" title="Units & reporting" kicker="05">

We report source–drain current in SI units (ampere, or a documented multiple), electrode baselines in the same run, and the residual as a dimensionless miR-223 load score until wet-lab calibration is complete.
